Project Overview

This is a Mining & Blasting Vibration Monitoring System - a comprehensive web-based dashboard designed for mining operations to monitor and analyze ground vibrations and air blast effects from controlled explosions. The system processes vibration data from Excel/CSV files and provides real-time visualization, statistical analysis, and filtering capabilities for safety compliance and operational optimization.

Primary Objectives

Monitor ground vibrations and air blast effects, ensure safety compliance with industry standards, provide real-time data visualization, and enable operational optimization through comprehensive analytics.

Critical Data Structure

Each processed record must contain: Row number, PPV value, Air Blast value, Blast date and time, Location data (bench name, zone name), Explosive data (total, column charge, base weight), and Drilling parameters (hole depth, spacing, burden).
